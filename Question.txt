MEDIA AND INFORMATION LITERACY (MIL) - FINAL EXAMINATION

QUESTION 1:
Which of the following best defines Information Literacy?
A. The ability to record high-definition video clips using mobile devices.
B. The ability to recognize when information is needed, and locate, evaluate, and effectively use it.
C. The skill of typing quickly on a physical or touch keyboard.
D. The capacity to configure network hardware and internet routers.
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 2:
What term refers to the primary channels of communication through which news, entertainment, education, and data are disseminated?
A. Media
B. Software
C. Bandwidth
D. Protocol
ANSWER: A

QUESTION 3:
Which type of media relies on paper publication and physical distribution, such as newspapers, magazines, and books?
A. Broadcast Media
B. Print Media
C. Digital Media
D. Streaming Media
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 4:
Which term describes the convergence of traditional media outlets with digital platforms and computer networks?
A. Analog Broadcast
B. Media Convergence
C. Frequency Modulation
D. Telecommunication Compression
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 5:
What critical skill allows an individual to analyze, evaluate, and create messages in a wide variety of forms?
A. Media Literacy
B. Computer Programming
C. Graphic Rendering
D. Network Administration
ANSWER: A

QUESTION 6:
What form of copyright license allows creators to grant public permission to use their creative work under specific conditions?
A. Patent Right
B. Creative Commons
C. Trade Secret
D. Proprietary Encryption
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 7:
Which of the following describes "Digital Divide"?
A. The speed difference between fiber optic and satellite internet connection.
B. The gap between demographic groups that have access to modern information and communications technology and those that do not.
C. The division of code into frontend and backend programming frameworks.
D. The separation of television signals into high-definition and standard definition channels.
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 8:
Which law protects original works of authorship, such as literature, music, and art, automatically from the moment of creation?
A. Trademark Law
B. Copyright Law
C. Patent Act
D. Antitrust Legislation
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 9:
What is the term for presenting someone else's work, ideas, or words as your own without proper attribution?
A. Plagiarism
B. Citation
C. Fair Use
D. Public Domain
ANSWER: A

QUESTION 10:
Under copyright law, what doctrine permits limited use of copyrighted material without permission for purposes such as criticism, news reporting, teaching, or research?
A. Intellectual Monopolization
B. Fair Use
C. Open Source Protocol
D. Digital Rights Enforcement
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 11:
Which of the following is considered an ethical guideline for online behavior, often referred to as "Netiquette"?
A. Sharing personal passwords with close friends for security backup.
B. Respecting others' privacy and avoiding hate speech or harassment.
C. Downloading pirated software using peer-to-peer networks.
D. Posting unverified claims anonymously to generate discussion.
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 12:
What term refers to false or misleading information that is spread intentionally to deceive people?
A. Misinformation
B. Disinformation
C. Malinformation
D. Uninformed News
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 13:
What is false information spread regardless of whether there is intent to deceive?
A. Misinformation
B. Disinformation
C. Defamation
D. Propaganda
ANSWER: A

QUESTION 14:
Which of the following is a key characteristic of reliable news sources?
A. Anonymous authors without credentials or contact info.
B. Sensationalized clickbait headlines designed to shock readers.
C. Editorial standards, transparent sources, and fact-checking processes.
D. Exclusive reliance on unsourced social media rumors.
ANSWER: C

QUESTION 15:
What media era began with the invention of the printing press by Johannes Gutenberg in the 15th century?
A. Industrial / Print Era
B. Pre-Industrial Era
C. Electronic Era
D. Information / Digital Era
ANSWER: A

QUESTION 16:
Which of the following technologies dominated the Electronic Age of media?
A. Clay Tablets and Papyrus
B. Radio, Television, and Transistor Circuits
C. Artificial Intelligence and Cloud Computing
D. Stone Carvings and Cave Paintings
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 17:
What is an "Echo Chamber" in the context of digital social media algorithms?
A. An audio filter used in podcast recording software.
B. An environment where a person only encounters information that reflects and reinforces their own opinions.
C. A network latency issue causing voice delays in video conferencing.
D. A public discussion forum where all opposing viewpoints are given equal broadcasting time.
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 18:
What is the primary function of a search engine algorithm?
A. To generate original creative novels automatically.
B. To index web pages and rank search results based on relevance and authority.
C. To prevent computer viruses from infecting client operating systems.
D. To convert text documents into audio recordings.
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 19:
Which term refers to the trail of data you create while using the internet, including visited websites, emails sent, and information submitted online?
A. Digital Footprint
B. Cyber Footprint
C. IP Log
D. Web Cache
ANSWER: A

QUESTION 20:
What security measure uses two different authentication factors to verify a user's identity before granting account access?
A. Single Sign-On (SSO)
B. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
C. Biometric Encryption
D. Password Hashing
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 21:
Which type of cyber threat involves deceptive emails or websites designed to trick users into revealing sensitive credentials or financial details?
A. Phishing
B. Ransomware
C. Trojan Horse
D. Keylogging
ANSWER: A

QUESTION 22:
What type of media content allows users to interact actively with the platform (e.g., polls, comment sections, video games, online quizzes)?
A. Passive Media
B. Interactive Media
C. Static Media
D. Linear Broadcast
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 23:
What is "Citizen Journalism"?
A. News coverage conducted exclusively by government press secretaries.
B. The collection, dissemination, and analysis of news and information by the general public using mobile devices and web platforms.
C. Journalism practiced only by citizens holding accredited university degrees in communications.
D. Corporate broadcast networks operating in capital cities.
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 24:
What term describes software designed specifically to gain unauthorized access, cause harm, or steal information from a computer system?
A. Firmware
B. Malware
C. Shareware
D. Middleware
ANSWER: B

QUESTION 25:
Why is critical thinking essential when consuming information on social media?
A. Because social media platforms guarantee 100% verified news accuracy.
B. Because anyone can publish content online without peer review or editorial oversight.
C. Because social media content cannot be shared or forwarded.
D. Because digital algorithms automatically filter out all bias and false claims.
ANSWER: B
